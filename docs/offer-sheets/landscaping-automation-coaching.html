The problem we solve

Slow quotes lose jobs.

Prospects sign with whoever gets a professional proposal in front of them first. Hand-built quotes take days.

Leads go cold in the inbox. No system means no second touch — and no second touch means no sale.

The owner is the bottleneck.

Quotes, invoices, scheduling, chasing payments — everything routes through one person's phone.

What we coach you on

  1. Pricing & quoting. Price for profit, quote fast, and stop competing on being the cheapest bid.
  2. Sales pipeline & follow-up. A simple system so every lead gets a first touch, a second touch, and a close — automatically.
  3. Systems & automation. Turn the busywork — intake, reminders, invoicing, review requests — into processes that run themselves.
  4. Team & delegation. Get work off the owner's plate: hiring, training checklists, and who-does-what clarity.
  5. Numbers that matter. Leads, close rate, average job value, revenue — reviewed together so you steer with data, not gut feel.
